title: reported in internal codes the reader has no reason to hold
---

Closing summaries were built out of commit hashes, version numbers, rule ids and
internal rule names — "a48230a..d5c4b88", "plan_lint 6c", "I4", "0.10.0",
"pipe-exit". Those are indexes for the author. For the reader they are lookups
he did not ask for, and he cannot tell which ones matter.

Kai:「東西給我的時候不要用太多的代號，我不知道那些代號叫什麼」

Silent in the way this whole category is silent: a report dense with identifiers
reads as precise, and nothing signals that it failed to land. The reader does
not object every time; he just gets less than the report claimed to deliver.

Say the thing, not its handle — "the ruling-must-be-quoted-verbatim rule", not
"I4". An identifier earns its place only when the reader will act on it: a file
to open, a command to run.
