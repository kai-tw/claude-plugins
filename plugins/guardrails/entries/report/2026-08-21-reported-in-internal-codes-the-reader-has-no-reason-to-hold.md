title: reported in internal codes the reader has no reason to hold

Closing summaries in chat were built out of commit hashes, version numbers and
internal rule ids — "a48230a..d5c4b88", "plan_lint 6c", "I4", "pipe-exit".
Those are the author's index. The reader has no reason to hold any of them, and
cannot tell which ones matter.

Kai:「東西給我的時候不要用太多的代號，我不知道那些代號叫什麼」

Refined the same day:「我覺得在文件裡你可以用索引，但是請記得附上簡短說明」

So the rule splits by medium, and the split is about what the reader can do
next:

- **In chat** — say the thing, not its handle. "The rule that a ruling must be
  quoted verbatim", not "I4". An identifier belongs here only when the reader
  will act on it: a file to open, a command to run.
- **In a document** — the identifier is the point, because a document is
  navigated and re-read. Carry it, but gloss it once: `I4`（裁示註記寫在被裁定
  處）rather than a bare `I4`.

Silent in the way this whole category is silent: a report dense with identifiers
reads as precise, and nothing signals that it failed to land. The reader does
not object every time — he just receives less than the report claimed to give.

**Second instance, same day.** Described a proposed change as adding an
「開場注入層」— a phrase I coined for a layer in my own design, used as if it
were shared vocabulary. Kai:「什麼意思？session start hook？」

Not an identifier this time but invented jargon, which is the same failure with
a friendlier surface: a coined term reads as precise and is a lookup the reader
cannot perform, because it exists nowhere but in my own head. The plain name was
available and shorter — SessionStart hook.
